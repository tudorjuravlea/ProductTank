# stat: a number with its caption

**Structure.** `<div data-slot="stat"><p class="stat-number">12</p><p class="subheading">talks in 2026</p></div>`,
repeated up to three times per canvas, or one big number on a square carousel card.

**Exact values.** MEASURED (numbers carousel): title ≈ 128 px bold, three lines, pitch 118; MTP wordmark
top-left. INFERRED: number 256 px, caption 36 px, 24 px gap; a `venniverse-double-bump` top and a
`venniverse-chevron` row bottom-left, `markup-plus` top-right.

**Colour slots.** `text1` numbers and captions; shapes `blurple` and `zingy-cyan`; markup `action`.

**Copy.** Numbers only from the brief or BRAND-FACTS §9. Captions in sentence case, max five words.

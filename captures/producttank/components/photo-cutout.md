# photo-cutout: subject and textural photography

**Structure.** Subject: `<figure class="photo subject" data-slot="photo"><img src="…" alt="…"></figure>`,
a transparent-background cut-out anchored bottom-right (or right), bleeding off at least one edge, layer 1
over the venn. Textural: `<figure class="photo textural" data-slot="photo">` full-bleed with the tritone wash
applied by `base.css` (Midnight › Bold Cyan › Zingy Cyan).

**Exact values.** MEASURED: portrait cut-out occupies roughly the right 45 % and the bottom 80 %; landscape
the right 40 %; spotlight the right 45 % with the head at ≈ 30 % height. Never smaller than a third of the
canvas height. Slides: textural photo on the right half of the cover.

**Colour slots.** None on subject photos (no colour wash). Textural photos take the tritone wash only.

**Copy.** Alt text names the person or place as the brief gives it.

**Notes.** The photo never covers the lockup, the headline or the markup. The venn never masks the photo.
Photos come from the brief (event photos, consented headshots); the Figma reference photography is never reused (DEC-007).

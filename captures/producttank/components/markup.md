# markup: the yellow highlighter gesture

**Structure.** `.markup` (box behind a word, CSS mask `shapes/markup-box.svg`), `.underline-markup`
(under a phrase), or an inline `<svg data-derived-art data-markup="sparkle|plus|arrow|circle|exclamation">`
placed near the subject. Exactly one markup gesture per canvas.

**Exact values.** MEASURED: box 406×107 around a 313×72 word on portrait (≈ 15 % padding each side);
underline 580×15 under a 52 px subtitle (story); sparkle 108×136 at the photo's shoulder (spotlight);
plus 218×90 top-right and arrow bottom-right (numbers carousel).

**Colour slots.** `action` (Markup Yellow) only. Text on the box: `ink` (Midnight) or black.

**Copy.** A markup box holds one to three words, never a sentence (Brand Guide 6.5).

**Notes.** Yellow never appears as a ground or as a large shape; CTA buttons may use it on web surfaces only.

# headline: statement + highlighted key word

**Structure.** `<h1 class="headline" data-slot="headline">Welcome back: <span class="markup" data-markup="box">Brussels</span></h1>`
inside `.copy`. The key word may sit on its own line (the renders break after the colon).

**Exact values.** MEASURED: portrait headline ≈ 100 px (cap band 72 px), x = 63, y = 75; line pitch to the
markup line 128 px; markup box 406×107 around a 313×72 word. Square ≈ 128 px, pitch 136. Landscape ≈ 82 px.
Story title ≈ 114 px, pitch 125. Max 2 lines of headline plus the key word; max 6 words before the key word.

**Type.** `display` role (Montserrat 700, letter-spacing −0.01 em, line-height 1.1). Sentence case, never
uppercase, never Capitalised Words (Brand Guide 3.2).

**Colour slots.** `text1` (white) on `background`; the markup word in `ink` (Midnight) on `action` (Markup Yellow).

**Copy.** One message. The key word is the city, the date word ("Tomorrow"), the theme or the call
("Speak"). No punctuation inside the markup. No em dashes.

**Notes.** INFERRED: on Midnight grounds the same rules hold; the markup stays yellow with Midnight text.

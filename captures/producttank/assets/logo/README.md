# Lockups

The files here are **placeholders drawn for this repository**: a dashed frame that says where the
ProductTank city lockup goes. They are not the logo and must not ship on a real post.

Get the real files from Mind the Product's ProductTank logo repository (ask your regional
coordinator): `ProductTank-logo-<City>-midnight.svg` and `-white.svg`. Save them here as
`producttank-<city>-midnight.svg` and `producttank-<city>-white.svg`, set `"file"` in
`../../lockup.json` to `producttank-<city>`, and regenerate the templates (README, "Your city's
lockup"). The brand rules for the lockup are in `../../components/lockup.md`: white or midnight only,
left-aligned, top or bottom corner, at least 80 px wide, never retyped, recoloured or rotated.

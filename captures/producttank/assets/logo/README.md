# Lockups

`producttank-brussels-midnight.svg` and `producttank-brussels-white.svg` are the official ProductTank
Brussels lockups, and `mtp-wordmark-*.svg` the Mind the Product wordmark, included with Mind the
Product's permission for ProductTank organisers (see `../../../../NOTICE`). `producttank-generic-*`,
`producttank-cityname-*` and `producttank-wordmark-*` are the generic variants from the same
repository. The `*-placeholder-*` files are dashed slots drawn for this repository, used only while a
city has no file yet.

For another city: get `ProductTank-logo-<City>-midnight.svg` and `-white.svg` from Mind the
Product's ProductTank logo repository (ask your regional coordinator), save them here as
`producttank-<city>-midnight.svg` and `producttank-<city>-white.svg`, set `"file"` in
`../../lockup.json` to `producttank-<city>`, and regenerate the templates (README, "Your city's
lockup"). The rules: white or midnight only, left-aligned, top or bottom corner, at least 80 px wide,
never retyped, recoloured or rotated (`../../components/lockup.md`).

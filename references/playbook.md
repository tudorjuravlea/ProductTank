# Playbook: which post, when, with which caption

Built from the organisers' own event checklist (Drive: *Checklist prior to / after the event*),
MTP's organiser rules (co-branding doc), the growth guide and the brand voice. Facts in examples
are placeholders; the skill never invents a real one (DEC-006).

## The event lifecycle and its posts

| When | Post | Archetype × format | Channel | Must carry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3–4 weeks before, Meetup page published | Announcement | `event-announce` portrait (LinkedIn/Instagram), landscape (LinkedIn link card, Meetup photo), square (WhatsApp); or `event-promo` landscape/portrait when the talk title, host logo and speakers should carry the post (MTP's organiser template, Black ground) | LinkedIn page, Meetup, WhatsApp group | date with month spelled out, time, venue, "Free to attend", "RSVP on Meetup" |
| Same week | Speaker spotlight, one per speaker | `speaker-spotlight` portrait or landscape | LinkedIn (tag the speaker) | name, role, talk title from the speaker's own bio |
| 2 weeks before | Line-up | `lineup` portrait / square | LinkedIn, Instagram | both speakers, facts row |
| Host confirmed | Host thanks / venue | `call-for` square adapted ("Thanks to our host") or `event-announce` with the venue as key word | LinkedIn | host name exactly as they write it; "hosted by" or "supported by", never "powered by" |
| 48–36 h before | Reminder / last call | `reminder` portrait + story | LinkedIn, Instagram story, WhatsApp, Meetup message | "RSVP on Meetup"; ask people to free their spot if they cannot come (waitlist) |
| Day of | Story from the room | `event-announce-story` or `reminder-story` with a live photo | Instagram / LinkedIn stories | nothing more than the fact of the evening |
| Day after | Recap + thanks + photos | `recap-thanks` portrait / landscape | LinkedIn (photos as a carousel), Meetup page | numbers only if counted, thanks to speakers and host, where slides and photos live |
| Between events | Call for speakers / hosts / sponsors | `call-for` portrait / square | LinkedIn, WhatsApp | how to reach the organisers (Meetup message), no URL on the canvas |
| Quarterly / yearly | Numbers, survey | `stat` square (carousel), `quote` portrait | LinkedIn, Instagram | numbers from the survey or Meetup stats, quoted with consent |
| World Product Day (May) | WPD post | `event-announce-story` (Midnight ground) + portrait | all | MTP's WPD assets when provided by MTP |

Cadence advice from the growth guide: a regular rhythm (every 2 months) beats bursts; ask for
hosts and speakers at every event and in every recap; word of mouth is the lever, social media
amplifies it; pre- and post-event engagement (comments, photos, anticipation) keeps it going.

## Caption skeleton (the text of the post, delivered beside the canvas)

1. **Hook** (one line, sentence case, moxie allowed): the theme or the promise, not "we are
   excited to announce".
2. **Facts** (one line each): day and date with the month spelled out, time, venue and city.
3. **Why come** (one or two sentences): who speaks, what they will share, who it is for
   ("product people", whatever the role).
4. **Call to action** (one): "RSVP on Meetup" plus the link; free to attend; bring a colleague.
5. **Credits**: speakers tagged, host thanked ("hosted by"), sponsors ("supported by").
6. **Hashtags** (3–5): `#ProductTank #ProductTankBrussels #mindtheproduct #productmanagement`
   plus the theme. This set is a proposal (rung 6); replace it with the community's own once
   confirmed and record it in BRAND-FACTS §9.

Length: LinkedIn ≤ 900 characters before the fold; Instagram ≤ 600; WhatsApp ≤ 500 with the link
in the first lines (a long message collapses behind "Read more").

## Rules that come from MTP, not from taste

- Events are free to attend; registration happens on Meetup.com (a second registration elsewhere
  is allowed as a co-registration).
- ProductTank branding must appear on every co-branded event; co-brand only with communities
  that do not compete with Mind the Product (no conferences in the same city, no product
  training providers).
- The talks are product related and never promote a product; say so to speakers and hosts.
- Speaker names, photos and titles come from the speakers themselves (they send bios, photos and
  titles per the checklist); never scrape or guess them.

## Photos

Subject photos (speakers, hosts) are cut-outs the speaker consented to; event photos are used
textural with the wash or plain in a carousel. Never take photography from the Figma reference
renders or from mindtheproduct.com (DEC-007). Keep faces of attendees out of stories unless they
agreed.

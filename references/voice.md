# Voice: how ProductTank Brussels sounds

Source: Brand Guide 2024 §1.2 Personality, §1.3 Tone of voice, §1.4 Design principles (captured
verbatim in `captures/producttank/BRAND-FACTS.md` §7). The lock's `content.voiceChart` carries
the same rules as do/don't pairs; the lints enforce the mechanical ones.

**Personality:** Authentic, Curious, Passionate, Welcoming, Connected, Moxie.

**Tone:** proper but informal. Puns and jokes are fine. Never authoritative, never "the definitive
guide". Humour that punches at the craft, not at people.

**Words**
- "Mind the Product" and "ProductTank", always spelled that way (never Product Tank, MindTheProduct).
- The audience is "product people" (singular: "product person"), never capitalised. Never "PM" or
  "PMs" unless quoting a title. "product manager" in full when the role matters.
- "meetup", one word; "RSVP on Meetup" is the call to action.
- UK spelling by default (organise, colour, programme); either is fine if consistent within a piece.
- Gender neutral: they, them, the speaker, the host.
- Neutral point of view for the community voice; signed opinions belong to the person who holds them.

**Dates and times:** spell the month out, always: "Tuesday 14 October", never "14/10". 24 h time
("18:30"). Year only when it is not obvious.

**Headlines:** sentence case, one message. Caps only in the label pill and the text highlight.
The Oxford comma stays ("speakers, hosts, and sponsors").

**One message, one call to action** per canvas and per post. Negative space is content. Keep it
simple; if the copy needs a second sentence to be understood, the first one is wrong.

**Do / don't** (from the lock)
- Welcoming: "Whatever your role in product, you are welcome. Free to attend, as always." ✓
  "Exclusive evening for senior PMs only." ✗
- Curious: "What did shipping to 2 million users teach Sofie about saying no? Come and find out." ✓
  "Learn the 5 secret hacks every PM must know." ✗
- Moxie: "Two talks, one room, zero slides about synergy." ✓
  "The definitive guide to product management, presented by experts." ✗
- Clear: "Tuesday 14 October, 18:30, at Venue name. RSVP on Meetup." ✓
  "Join us soon for an amazing event, details to follow, link in bio, don't miss out!" ✗
- Connected: "Thanks to our host and to everyone who stayed for the networking." ✓
  "Another successful event delivered by the organising committee." ✗
